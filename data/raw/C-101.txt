Contract ID: C-101
Title: QuickHire Staffing Agreement
Parties: Northstar Solutions Ltd. and QuickHire Staffing Ltd.

1. PAYMENT TERMS
Undisputed invoices are due within 45 calendar days of receipt. A late fee of 2% per month applies to overdue balances.

2. TERMINATION
Either party may end this Agreement for convenience with 20 days written notice. If either party breaches the Agreement, the breaching party has 5 business days to correct the issue before the other party may terminate.

3. AUTOMATIC RENEWAL
This Agreement renews automatically for successive 6-month periods unless the Customer gives 45 days written notice before the renewal date.

4. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
Each party's liability for ordinary claims is capped at the fees paid in the preceding 12 months. This cap does not apply to breaches of confidentiality or to fraud.
