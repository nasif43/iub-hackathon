Contract ID: C-105
Title: Meridian Cloud Hosting Agreement
Parties: Northstar Solutions Ltd. and Meridian Hosting Ltd.

2.1 Payment
The Customer must settle undisputed invoices within 4 weeks of receipt.

4.1 Termination
Either party may terminate for convenience by giving 6 weeks written notice. A party in breach has 2 business weeks to cure the breach before termination.

5.1 Automatic Renewal
The Agreement renews automatically for a further 2 years unless the Customer gives 6 weeks written notice before the renewal date.

7.1 Data Protection
Personal data is encrypted in transit only. Confirmed breaches are reported within 2 business days. Subprocessors may be appointed without prior approval. Data is deleted or returned within one quarter after the service ends.

9.1 Limitation of Liability
Meridian's total liability for ordinary claims is capped at fees paid during the previous 2 quarters. No carve-outs are stated.
