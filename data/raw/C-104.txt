Contract ID: C-104
Title: Harborview Facilities Management Agreement
Parties: Northstar Solutions Ltd. and Harborview FM Services Ltd.

A.1 Governing Law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the jurisdiction in which the Customer is registered.

A.2 Payment
The Customer must pay undisputed invoices within 60 calendar days after receipt and acceptance of the related service. A late fee of 3% per month applies.

A.3 Force Majeure
Neither party is liable for delays caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including natural disasters and government action.

A.4 Termination
Either party may terminate immediately if the other party becomes insolvent. For all other terminations for convenience, either party must give 45 days written notice.

A.5 Assignment
Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other party's written consent.

A.6 Confidentiality
Both parties must protect each other's confidential information and use it only for this Agreement. This duty continues for 18 months after the Agreement ends. There are no stated carve-outs for public or independently developed information.
